Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Creative Workspace Research is a personal content management application used by its owner to create and schedule original content, manage boards and Pins, and review analytics for the owner's own Pinterest account.

Data accessed

The application may access the authenticated owner's Pinterest profile, boards, Pins, Pin analytics, and publishing permissions granted through Pinterest OAuth.

How data is used

Data is used only to research, prepare, publish, schedule, verify, and measure the owner's own content. The application does not sell personal data, build advertising profiles for third parties, or share Pinterest account data with unrelated parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and operational records are kept in the owner's private environment. Access is limited to the owner and may be revoked through Pinterest account settings.

Data retention and deletion

Operational records are retained only as needed to manage and measure the owner's content. The owner may revoke access and delete retained records at any time.
